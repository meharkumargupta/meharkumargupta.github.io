A little about my journey.

Every journey begins somewhere. Mine is shaped by my roots, the people and places around me, and a lasting curiosity to understand more.

A moment along the way

Grounded in my roots, looking ahead.

I come from a middle-class family background in Bhokraha Rural Municipality, Sunsari District, Nepal. My hometown is an important part of my story and the foundation of the person I am becoming.

At present, I am living in Biratnagar for my education. I speak Maithili as my mother tongue, communicate fluently in Nepali, and continue improving my conversational English.

I enjoy reading, exploring technology and biological science, following cricket and learning about the share market. Above all, I value patience, hard work, continuous learning and personal development.
