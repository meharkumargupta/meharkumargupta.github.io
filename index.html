About Me

A quiet curiosity, carried from the village to the city

My background is from Bhokraha Rural Municipality in Sunsari District, Nepal, where I grew up in a middle-class family. Today I live in Biratnagar, where I am continuing my education.

I enjoy learning new things, reading widely, and understanding how technology and biological science work. I believe in continuous learning, patience, personal development and hard work, and I try to apply those values to whatever I take on.
